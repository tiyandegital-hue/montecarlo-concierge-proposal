Privacy Policy.

Plain language, because this is a discretion business. This page says what we collect from the request form and why — nothing more.

What we collect

When you make a request or apply for membership, we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, WhatsApp phone number, dates, party size, destination and the details of your brief. That is all we ask for, and it is all we need to handle the request.

Why we collect it

To handle your request: to confirm availability, to contact you about it, and to arrange what you asked for. We do not use your details for anything else.

What we never do

  • No third-party advertising — your details are never sold or shared for advertising.
  • No commercial broker sharing — your brief is not passed to brokers.
  • No analytics selling — we do not sell browsing or usage data.

Retention and erasure

Itineraries are archived under encrypted cold storage and deleted upon request. You have the right to erasure: contact us at montecarloconcierge1@gmail.com or on WhatsApp +33 6 33 96 71 92 and ask for your data to be deleted, and it will be purged.

Contact

Questions about your data: email montecarloconcierge1@gmail.com or WhatsApp +33 6 33 96 71 92. This policy is governed by the data-protection law applicable in Monaco and the EU (GDPR).
